Who We Are

We Don't Just
Produce Events.
We Own Them.

GUSAR Entertainment is Miami's full-service event agency — built around a single promise: complete ownership of your event from first call to final breakdown.

Our in-house team covers every discipline — event management, technical production, artistic talent, marketing, and logistics — so nothing falls through the cracks. We scale from a $500 AV setup to a multi-day, large-format production without changing a single point of contact.

Based in South Florida and expanding globally, we bring the same obsessive attention to detail to every project — regardless of size, budget, or complexity.
